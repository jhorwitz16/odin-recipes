Slow Cooker Lasagna

Home Image of Slow Cooker Lasagna alt="Picture of slow cooker lasagna" height="100" width="100">

This slow cooker lasagna layers browned ground beef in a seasoned tomato sauce with uncooked lasagna noodles and a mixture of mozzarella, cottage, and Parmesan cheeses, all assembled directly in the slow cooker. It cooks slowly over several hours, allowing the noodles to soften and the flavors to meld into a rich, cheesy, and hearty one-pot version of classic lasagna.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 1 (29 ounce) can tomato sauce
  • 1 (6 ounce) can tomato paste
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 16 ounces shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 12 ounces cottage cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 (12 ounce) package lasagna noodles

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Cook ground beef, onion, and garlic in a large skillet over medium heat until the meat is browned.
  3. Add tomato sauce, tomato paste, salt, and oregano and stir until well combined and heated through.
  4. Stir mozzarella, cottage cheese, and Parmesan together in a large bowl.
  5. Spoon a layer of the meat mixture onto the bottom of a slow cooker.
  6. Add a double layer of uncooked lasagna noodles, breaking noodles to fit into cooker as needed.
  7. Top noodles with a portion of cheese mixture.
  8. Repeat the layering of sauce, noodles, and cheese until all the ingredients are used.
  9. Cover and cook on Low until lasagna noodles are tender, about 4 to 6 hours.
